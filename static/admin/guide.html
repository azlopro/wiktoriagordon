Editing your website

Choose a section, make the change, preview it and save.

For every change

  1. Edit both DA and EN where language tabs appear.
  2. Check the visual preview beside the form.
  3. Save, wait 1–2 minutes and check both live pages.

Reviewing translations

Review all text shows the Danish and English wording side by side. Sign in there, edit directly in the text fields and use the single Save button.

Prices, durations, images and genuine customer quotes are shared between the languages and may intentionally be identical.

Website and bookings are separate

This editor changes website content. Appointments, days off and blocked times are managed in the booking calendar once that setup is complete.

If something is wrong

Stop making further edits and tell the site administrator what you changed and roughly when. Every save has version history and can be restored.

Domain, login, layout and payment settings are intentionally not editable here.
