Every change

  1. Choose the section.
  2. Edit DA and EN where tabs appear.
  3. Check the visual preview.
  4. Save and check the live pages.

Publishing normally takes 1–2 minutes. Prices, durations, images, opening hours and genuine review quotes are shared between the languages. Translatable wording has separate DA and EN tabs.

The preview is a safety check, not the published website. After saving, open both the Danish page and English page.

Where is it?

  • Treatments & pricesNames, descriptions, duration, price, order and highlighted treatment.
  • Contact & opening hoursRegular weekly hours, public street, phone, email and social links.
  • Photos used on the websiteLogo, large top photo and About photo.
  • Before & after sliderResult pairs and the treatment label under each pair.
  • Main page textHeadings and paragraphs across the homepage.
  • Google & link previewsGoogle title and description, plus the image shown when the site is shared.

Unused result photos are kept safely in the editor but are not currently shown. Visitors are sent to Instagram for more results.

Website vs. bookings

This editor changes what visitors see on the website. Appointments, days off and individual blocked times belong in the booking calendar. Once the booking setup is finished, a direct Bookings & availability shortcut should be added to the top of the editor.

Changing regular opening hours here changes the public information on the website. Blocking one afternoon in the calendar does not change the public opening-hours text; it only removes those appointment times.

Photos

Upload sharp JPEG, PNG or WebP images only when the client has allowed website use. The editor converts large phone photos to efficient WebP files automatically.

Before and after images should use the same angle, distance and lighting. Otherwise the slider will appear to jump when visitors move it.

Recovery

Sign in with Wiktoria’s own GitHub account. The first time, GitHub may ask her to approve access to the website repository. She should never share that password; another editor gets their own account and access.

Save publishes the change. The arrow beside Save also offers Save without publishing for unfinished work. Every save has version history.

If something looks wrong: stop making further edits and tell the site administrator which section you changed and roughly when. The previous version can be restored.

Domain settings, login, layout code, the payment connection and booking integration are intentionally not editable here because they require testing before publication.
