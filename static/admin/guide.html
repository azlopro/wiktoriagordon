A simple guide to your website

Most changes only take a minute. Choose the relevant section, make the edit, check the preview and save.

The safe three-step routine

  1. Change both DA and EN wherever language tabs appear.
  2. Use the preview beside the form to catch mistakes before saving.
  3. Save, wait 1–2 minutes, then open the live website and check both languages.

The things you will use most

  • Treatments & prices controls treatment cards, durations and the price list.
  • Contact & opening hours controls public contact details and regular weekly hours.
  • Website photos controls the large page photos.
  • Customer reviews and Questions & answers can be reordered by dragging.

Changing the top photo

The desktop/tablet and phone versions are separate on purpose. Use a wide landscape photo for desktop and tablet, and an upright or nearly square crop for phones. Always check both after saving.

Reviewing both languages

Review wording shows Danish and English side by side. You can edit directly in the text fields and save all wording changes together.

Prices, durations and images are shared between the languages and may intentionally be identical.

Customer quotes are written once and kept in the language the client wrote them in. Each quote has a language field: Danish quotes appear on the Danish site, English quotes on the English site. Leave it empty and the quote appears on both.

Website and bookings are separate

This editor changes website content. Appointments, days off and blocked times are managed in the booking calendar once that setup is complete.

If something looks wrong

Do not make more changes to compensate for it. Tell the site administrator what you changed and roughly when. Every save has version history and can be restored.

Domain, login, layout and payment settings are intentionally not editable here.
